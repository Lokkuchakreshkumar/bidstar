# Cinebid Design Guidelines

Please always read and strictly adhere to [design.md](file:///home/chakresh/projects/cinebid/design.md) and the `.agents/skills/vercel-brand-guidelines/SKILL.md` skill for all UI, components, layouts, typography, spacing, and theme work.

- **Dark Mode**: Must use the Anthropic / Cloud-like dark palette (warm dark charcoal `#161514`, elevated surfaces `#1c1b1a` / `#22211f`, restrained borders `#2e2c29`, soft ivory `#f4efe9`, warm stone `#9c9489`). Never use pure pitch black (`#000000`).
- **Typography**: Use Geist Sans and Geist Mono. Use tabular numerals (`tabular-nums`) on all amounts, ranks, and numbers.
- **Craft**: Maintain Vercel editorial craft, relational spacing, full-width evidence tables with aligned headers, and monochrome restraint with purposeful cinema flame accent (`#e95325`).
